# Linear Algebra

## Transpose

$$
(\mathbf{A}+\mathbf{B})^t=\mathbf{A}^t+\mathbf{B}^t\\
(\mathbf{AB})^t=\mathbf{B}^t\mathbf{A}^t\\
$$

## Inverse

$$
(\mathbf{AB})^{-1}=\mathbf{B}^{-1}\mathbf{A}^{-1}\\
(\mathbf{A}^{t})^{-1}=(\mathbf{A^{-1}})^t,\ \text{if $\mathbf{A}^{t}$ is invertible.}\\
(c\mathbf{A})^{-1}=c^{-1}\mathbf{A}^{-1},\ \text{if $c\ne0\ \&\ c\mathbf{A}$ is invertible.}\\
\\

\textbf{Formula:}\\
\mathbf{A}=\begin{bmatrix}
a&b\\c&d
\end{bmatrix}\\
\mathbf{A}^{-1}=\frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{bmatrix}
d&-b\\-c&a
\end{bmatrix}\\
$$

## Determinant

$$
\textbf{Special case: }\\
\text{if } 
\mathbf{A}=\begin{bmatrix}
a&b\\c&d
\end{bmatrix}\text{, then }
\det(\mathbf{A})=\begin{vmatrix}
a&b\\c&d
\end{vmatrix}=ad-bc
$$
